A little about me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in statistics at East China Normal University in Shanghai. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the same university in 2024 and spent part of my doctoral training at Technion – Israel Institute of Technology.

My research spans Bayesian statistics, statistical learning and optimization, biostatistics, and statistical computing. I am interested in how statistical models can represent uncertainty and guide decisions. Guided by the KISS principle—Keep It Simple and Smart—I develop methods that are simple, transparent, and practical to implement, together with R software that makes them accessible.
