About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in statistics at East China Normal University. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the same university in 2024.

My research interests include Bayesian statistics, statistical learning and optimization, and biostatistics, with a focus on kernel process models, Bayesian optimization, and adaptive clinical trial design. I also develop R software to make these methods accessible. See my publications and software.
