01 / Research

Research interests

Bayesian nonparametric modeling

Kernel-based modeling of binomial and multinomial probabilities, with conjugate updating and practical uncertainty quantification.

Bayesian optimization

Surrogate modeling and exact-penalty methods for expensive black-box optimization under constraints.

Clinical trial design

Bayesian dose-finding and phase I/II platform designs, including information borrowing and time-to-event outcomes.

Statistical computing

Reproducible methods and R software that connect statistical methodology with usable implementations.

03 / Software

Methods into R software

BKP

Beta Kernel Process Modeling

An R package for binomial and multinomial probability modeling with pointwise Beta and Dirichlet posterior summaries. Includes TwinBKP and TwinDKP for scalable global-local modeling.

WebsiteCRANGitHub

SKBD

Shared Keyboard Designs

Tools for phase I dose-finding: decision tables, operating-characteristic simulations, delayed toxicity outcomes, and adaptive dose insertion. Includes an interactive Shiny application.

CRANGitHub

EPBO

Bayesian Optimization via Exact Penalty

Research implementation of constrained Bayesian optimization using exact penalty functions. Companion code for the Technometrics paper.

GitHubPaper
